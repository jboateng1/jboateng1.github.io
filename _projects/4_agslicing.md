AgSlicing — _Predictable RAN and Spectrum Slicing for Precision Agriculture_ — is a multi-institution project funded under the NSF Verticals-enabling Intelligent Network Systems (VINES) program, Track 2 (verticals-driven technology development, demonstration, and translation). Precision agriculture increasingly relies on drones, autonomous machines, and edge AI that need wireless links with predictable latency and reliability, not just best-effort throughput, and they often need it across wide, sparsely served rural areas.

The project develops RAN and spectrum slicing techniques that give agricultural applications predictable, real-time network performance in NextG systems, and demonstrates the integrated system at scale in real farm settings. These are the conditions the ARA Wireless Living Lab was built for.

The team is led by Iowa State University and includes the University of Virginia, Skylark Wireless, Salin 247, and Federated Wireless, bringing together academic research, wireless industry, and agricultural technology partners.
